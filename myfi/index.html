My Fi.

Every account. One net worth.

MyFi is a self-hosted personal finance dashboard that unifies every account, tracks investments in real time, and projects wealth through retirement with Monte Carlo forecasting. It's currently invite-only while I dogfood it with real accounts — the interactive demo below runs on simulated data so you can see exactly what's inside.

Capabilities

What MyFi
can do.

Everything you need to see your whole financial picture in one place.

Dashboard

A high-level snapshot of net worth across every account, allocation by asset class and tax treatment, goal milestones, and a net-worth trend chart.

Accounts & Holdings

Track checking, savings, retirement, brokerage, and credit accounts in one place, plus live prices, cost basis, and unrealized gain/loss on individual securities.

Retirement Forecast

Monte Carlo projections from today to age 100, run as base, optimistic, and pessimistic scenarios that factor in contributions, returns, inflation, and Social Security.

Goals & Contributions

Set milestones like net-worth or down-payment targets and track progress automatically, while modeling recurring savings and employer match into the forecast.

Household Linking

Link your account with a partner's to combine financial data into a unified net-worth view and a shared retirement forecast.

CSV & OFX Import

Bulk-upload historical balances from a spreadsheet, or parse bank and brokerage OFX/QFX exports with institution detection and a diff preview before writing changes.

AI Chat

Ask natural-language questions about your finances via a locally-run LLM. No data ever leaves the machine.

Admin Panel

Generate invite codes, manage users, and inspect the referral tree — how MyFi's invite-only access is administered behind the scenes.
