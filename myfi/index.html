Capabilities

What MyFi
can do.

Everything needed to see a whole financial picture in one place — and plan what comes next.

Dashboard

A high-level snapshot of net worth across every account, allocation by asset class and tax treatment, and a net-worth trend chart. Fully customizable — show, hide, and reorder every card.

Accounts & Holdings

Track checking, savings, retirement, brokerage, and credit accounts in one place, plus live prices, cost basis, and unrealized gain/loss on individual securities.

Retirement Forecast

Monte Carlo projections from today to age 100, run as base, optimistic, and pessimistic scenarios that factor in contributions, returns, inflation, and Social Security.

Account Syncing

Connect a real brokerage, retirement, bank, or credit account via Plaid — holdings and balances sync automatically, with a daily refresh and a manual sync-now action for anything that needs an update sooner.

Scenario Planning

Model named what-if plans — retire earlier, save more, change the return assumption — and see each one compared side-by-side against the current baseline.

Goals & Contributions

Set milestones like net-worth or down-payment targets and track progress automatically, while modeling recurring savings and employer match into the forecast.

Household Linking

Link an account with a partner's to combine financial data into a unified net-worth view and a shared retirement forecast.

CSV & OFX Import

Bulk-upload historical balances from a spreadsheet, or parse bank and brokerage OFX/QFX exports with institution detection and a diff preview before writing changes.

AI Chat

Ask natural-language questions about your finances via a locally-run LLM. No data ever leaves the machine.
