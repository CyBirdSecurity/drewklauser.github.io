Capabilities

What MyFi
can do.

Everything needed to see a whole financial picture in one place — and plan what comes next.

Dashboard

A high-level snapshot of net worth across every account, allocation by asset class and tax treatment, and a net-worth trend chart. Fully customizable — show, hide, and reorder every card.

Accounts & Holdings

Track checking, savings, retirement, brokerage, and credit accounts in one place, plus live prices, cost basis, and unrealized gain/loss on individual securities.

Retirement Forecast

Monte Carlo projections from today to age 100, run as base, optimistic, and pessimistic scenarios that factor in contributions, returns, inflation, and Social Security.

Account Syncing

Connect brokerage, retirement, bank, and credit accounts through Plaid — balances, holdings, and transactions refresh daily, with a sync-now button when you need it sooner. Included on hosted; bring your own Plaid keys when self-hosting.

Scenario Planning

Model named what-if plans — retire earlier, save more, change the return assumption — and see each one compared side-by-side against the current baseline.

Goals & Contributions

Set milestones like net-worth or down-payment targets and track progress automatically, while modeling recurring savings and employer match into the forecast.

Household Planning

Plan as a couple: add a partner with their own income, retirement age, and Social Security, mark accounts as yours, theirs, or joint, and get one combined forecast.

CSV & OFX Import

Bulk-upload historical balances from a spreadsheet, or parse bank and brokerage OFX/QFX exports with institution detection and a diff preview before writing changes.

AI Assistance

A language model suggests categories for imported transactions — a local Ollama model when self-hosting, so nothing leaves your server. Hosted adds AI chat for plain-language questions about your finances.

Two Ways to Run MyFi

Same app.
You pick where it lives.

Both versions share the dashboard, forecasting, budgeting, and tax planning. The difference is who runs the server.

Hosted

Free during early access

MyFi Hosted

Sign in from any device and start planning. Nothing to install or keep running.

  • Bank sync included. Connect banks and brokerages through Plaid — no developer account or API keys.
  • Zero maintenance. No Docker, HTTPS, upgrades, or backups to manage. I run the servers.
  • Real accounts & email. Reset a password by email, and link a partner’s own login into one shared plan.
  • AI chat. Ask questions about your finances in plain language, with smarter transaction categorization.

Self-hosted

Open source · MIT

MyFi-OS

Run MyFi on your own hardware with Docker. Your finances never leave your machine.

  • Your data, your server. Everything lives in your own PostgreSQL database. No telemetry, no trackers.
  • Local AI by default. Category suggestions come from a local Ollama model; Anthropic is optional.
  • Bring your own Plaid. Bank sync is optional. Manual entry and CSV/OFX import work without it.
  • Free and MIT-licensed. Fork it, change it, and send improvements back.
$ git clone https://github.com/cybirdsecurity/myfi-os.git
$ cd myfi-os && cp .env.example .env
$ docker compose up -d --build
View on GitHub

Needs Docker and roughly 4 CPU cores / 8 GB RAM for the local model. Setup steps are in the README.
